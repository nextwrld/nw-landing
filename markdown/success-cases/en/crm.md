## Project Context

InmoCRM is an **MVP in development** based on applied research in software engineering (Final Degree Project).

The starting point was a recurring problem in Latin American real estate agencies: manual processes, isolated tools, and repetitive administrative work.

The goal of this stage is to design a platform that allows managing the real estate operation from a more centralized place, without promising results that are not yet verified.

## The Problem

The research identified common friction points in the sector:

- Manual property publishing across multiple channels
- Duplication of administrative tasks
- Lack of centralized metrics
- Generic CRMs not adapted to the real estate domain

The observed problem is not only technological, but one of process: the operation grew without a tool designed to support it.

## The Next Wrld Approach

We approached this stage as research and design, guided by three principles.

### Think in domains, not features

Each part of the real estate business was modeled as a domain: users, properties, listings, and metrics.

### Modular design from the start

The design proposes separating modules so the platform can evolve without costly rewrites.

### AI with judgment

Artificial intelligence is considered an optional component that adds value only when it improves a concrete task, not as a product requirement.

## Solution Design

An MVP was designed with these decisions:

- Modules separated by domain (users, properties, listings, metrics)
- Event-based communication
- Separation between operational data and queries
- Optional AI for property descriptions and recommendations

## Current Status

InmoCRM is at the **MVP in development** stage. Verified capabilities, results, and dates will be published once they are approved. This page does not describe a system in use or measured results.

## Research Learnings

- Architecture shapes the future speed of the business
- AI must be designed with structural judgment, not as a patch
- Systems must be designed to evolve
